# Handler Guide — Day to Day

> Owner: Claims Operations
> Last reviewed: November 2021

## New claim received

Record the claim the same day it arrives. You must log a telephone contact
attempt with the insured **within 2 working days** of receipt — the diary system
will chase you if you do not, and the SLA report counts it against the team.

## Panel solicitors

The referral limit is **£2,500**. On non-DAS business, any claim over £2,500 must
be instructed to a panel solicitor. If the insured has already instructed someone
off-panel, the claim is rejected and you write to them explaining why.

DAS business is exempt — do not apply this check to DAS files.

## Disbursements

A disbursement cannot be released until the parent claim is authorised. The
payment screen will show it as pending until then.

### Payment cutoff

The BACS file goes to the bank at **15:00**. Anything submitted after 15:00 takes
the next working day's value date. If a supplier is chasing, tell them the value
date, not the submission date.

### Rejections

A rejected disbursement can be corrected and resubmitted, but only **twice**.
After the second resubmission the item is closed and a new disbursement has to be
raised.

### Medical reports

Medical report fees sit outside the general fee arrangements — the 2015 panel
agreement fixes them separately. Do not query a medical fee against the general
schedule.
