# Claims Authority Policy

> Document ref: CAP-003 · Version 3.1 · Effective 1 January 2024
> Owner: Head of Claims · Approved by: Claims Governance Committee
> Applies to: all LEI claims, UK and CA markets, all brands

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out who may authorise a claim or a disbursement, the limits that
apply, and the service standards claims staff must meet. Where a system control
exists, the system must enforce this policy. Where it does not, the handler is
responsible for compliance.

## 2. Eligibility

2.1 **Lapsed cover.** A claim must be declined where the policy lapsed more than
**30 days** before the incident date. No discretion applies.

2.2 **Conflict of interest.** A claim must be declined where the insured and the
counterparty are the same party.

## 3. Authorisation limits

3.1 **Automatic authorisation.** UK claims with a settlement estimate below
**£1,000**, and CA claims below **CAD 750**, may be authorised by the system
without handler involvement, unless 3.3 applies.

3.2 **Senior sign-off.** Any claim with a settlement estimate above **£10,000**
must be signed off by a handler of seniority 4 or above before authorisation.
There are no exceptions to this requirement, including for system or batch users.

3.3 **Referral watchlist.** Claims introduced by the following brokers require
senior sign-off whatever their value: **BR0042, BR0117, BR0301, BR0388**.
Compliance maintains the list; changes take effect on publication.

3.4 **Second signature.** Any claim with a settlement estimate above **£50,000**
requires a second signature from the Claims Manager before authorisation.

## 4. Solicitors and disbursements

4.1 **Panel solicitors.** On non-DAS business, any claim, and any single
disbursement, above **£2,500** must be handled by a panel solicitor. A
disbursement above £2,500 on an off-panel file must not be paid.

4.2 **Counsel fees.** Counsel fees are capped at **£4,000** per disbursement.
Where a fee is reduced to the cap, the handler must be notified so they can
explain the reduction to counsel.

4.3 **Payment cutoff.** BACS payments submitted before **15:00** on a working day
are value-dated the same day. Later submissions take the next working day.

## 5. Service standards

5.1 **First contact.** A telephone contact attempt with the insured must be
logged within **2 working days** of a claim being received.

5.2 **Complaints.** Every complaint must be acknowledged in writing within
**5 working days**.

## 6. Timeliness

6.1 Eligible claims must be authorised within **10 working days** of receipt,
**all year round**. Workload or budget periods are not grounds for delay.

## 7. Review

This policy is reviewed annually by the Claims Governance Committee.
